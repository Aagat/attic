SAVE TO ATTIC

Your reading library,
one click away.

Connect once, then click the Attic toolbar button on any article. You can also right-click a link and choose “Save link to Attic”.

Use your Attic access key, separate from your ChatGPT login. It stays in this browser’s local extension storage and is sent only to your configured server.

Open your library ↗

Pin the extension using your browser’s Extensions menu. A ✓ badge means the article was queued; a ! badge means it failed. Hover over the icon for details.
